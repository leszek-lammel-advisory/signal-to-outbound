# Start here: 8 minutes

You'll leave this session with a list of companies and a signal email for each, ready to send. Start it now: it runs while we talk.

## 1. Open your AI (1 minute)

- **Have a Clay trial?** Use claude.ai with the Clay connector on (Settings, then Connectors, then Clay). When Claude asks to use a Clay tool, click **Always allow**, or the run stops and waits for you.
- **No Clay?** Use Claude or ChatGPT with web search on.
- **Claude Code or Codex?** Clone this repository, open it and type `start`.

## 2. Copy the prompt (1 minute)

- With Clay: [demo/prompt-with-clay.md](demo/prompt-with-clay.md)
- Without Clay: [version B of demo/prompt-no-tools.md](demo/prompt-no-tools.md#version-b-search-live). No campaign yet? [Version A](demo/prompt-no-tools.md#version-a-check-our-list-again-live) practises on our list of 20 companies.

## 3. Put in your campaign (5 minutes)

Replace the `MY CAMPAIGN` block at the top of the prompt with what you made today:

| Line | From |
|---|---|
| I sell, Buyers | Your campaign idea from this morning |
| The signal | The timing and buying signals session. No signal yet? Pick one from [the library](signals/library.md). |
| What usually goes wrong, Proof, Desired outcome, Objection, Lead magnet | Your messaging canvas |

No proof yet? Write `none`: the email then says "We help..." instead of inventing a number.

No campaign yet? Keep the example. Rampwise is made up, and the prompt works as it is.

## 4. Send it

It takes 3 to 10 minutes. Leave it running. After the talk you'll [finish your list](YOUR-TURN.md).

What to expect: with Clay, 5 to 10 companies, each with an email. Without Clay, often 1 to 5, because many job pages block AI readers and the prompt drops what it can't check.
